# Content plan

## Landing (`/`)
1. Hero: "Every great game port, one launcher." Subline: install, update and play community
   ports of classic games on Windows, macOS, Linux and Android — bring your own game files.
   CTA: Download for <detected OS> · secondary: How it works. To the right (below on
   narrow screens): a screenshot of the app's Discover page on the live catalog.
2. Feature grid: One-click install & updates · Playable status per OS · Cloud saves you own
   (encrypted, your Drive/Dropbox/S3) · Sign in with Bluesky or any AT Protocol account ·
   Discussion boards & chat · Steam Deck ready.
2b. "A look inside": app screenshots (game page, library, community, chat with an invite),
   each with a one-line caption; real catalog data, example people. Recapture with `pnpm screenshots` when the app's UI changes.
3. "How it works" strip: Find a port → Add your game files (checked on your device, never
   uploaded) → Play → Your saves follow you.
4. Credit the community: "Portside lists projects built by their own teams" + link to
   awesome-decomp-games and port maintainers; no game art.
5. Footer: GitHub, docs, content policy, privacy, terms, contact.

## Download (`/download`) — see downloads.md
## How it works (`/how-it-works`) — catalog source, asset import, save encryption, AT Protocol accounts, what's public.
## FAQ — Is this legal? Does Portside host games? Where are my saves? Can I use my Bluesky account? Why isn't it on Google Play / iOS?
## Help (`/help/*`) — install per OS, Steam Deck Game Mode, Android sideloading, recovering saves, recovery phrase, troubleshooting codes (mirror client error codes).
## Legal — privacy (Portside runs no server and stores nothing about users; public AT Protocol records are public; saves stay in the user's own storage), terms, content policy (no ROM requests), takedown contact for maintainers.
